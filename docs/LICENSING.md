# Licence and trial

Agent Sandboxes is a paid app with a free 30-day trial. **Licensing is soft: the app never disables a feature.**

## Without a key

Everything works. The app shows: `enter a licence key (30-day trial available)`.

## Get a trial key

Write to **sales@zyvor.dev** for a 30-day trial key. Choose **Licence…**, paste the key (it looks like `ZYV1.…`) and press **Verify and Save**. The key is checked on your Mac and nothing is sent anywhere. While a key is active the status line shows the days left; after expiry it says so. Nothing is switched off in any case.
